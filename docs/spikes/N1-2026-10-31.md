# Spike N1 — Landscape: Urdu/Roman-Urdu business-message parsing & Urdu ASR

_Access window: 2026-10-31 · researcher: spike-researcher · phase: M0_  
_No personal data, real chats, or secrets. Not legal advice._

## Verdict

**Gap open.** No open, tested Urdu/Roman-Urdu business-message parser plus field-level eval set was found that extracts order/payment/credit/delivery **records** (item, qty, unit, amount, direction, relative dates) with a deterministic number-grounding validator. Adjacent open assets exist (support-ticket triage, Hinglish e-commerce intents, Roman-Urdu sentiment, Urdu NER, ASR models with published WER), but they do not fill this wedge; product demos and vendor blogs that claim similar parsing are **not** reusable libraries with public eval and are labelled unverified below.

## Verified vs inferred

### Verified (from primary pages fetched or paper text)
- No primary card for an open **business-record** parser (order lines + money + udhaar + delivery dates) with a public field-level / silent-error eval was located under the search terms below.
- Closest open text assets are **support-ticket triage** (Roman Urdu/Urdu/English, MIT data) and **Hinglish e-commerce support** (SETU, MIT) — both intent/status oriented, not SME ledger capture.
- Roman Urdu open data is dominated by **sentiment**, not structured business NLU.
- Urdu ASR options with primary docs: Whisper (MIT), MMS and Seamless (CC-BY-NC). Published COLING 2025 WER numbers as in the table. Common Voice Urdu exists under CC0 via Mozilla Data Collective.
- Apps claiming Roman Urdu stock/khata parsing exist as GitHub/README or blog posts; they are not substitutes for an open eval set.

### Inferred (not proven)
- Commercial Urdu WhatsApp agents (Myyna, AdAxiom, etc.) likely keep NLU closed — consistent with PROJECT_CONTEXT §2/§3 but **not re-verified** in this spike (LinkedIn/vendor channels excluded as evidence).
- Hinglish SETU templates may transfer partially to Roman Urdu orthography but dialect/lexicon (udhaar, peti, derh) will differ — hypothesis only.
- Fine-tuned Whisper/Seamless will still need domain eval on compressed WA audio (deferred to N3).

## Open questions that change the decision

1. Does any closed-but-licensable Pakistani agency dataset exist that could be anonymised under D4/D5, or must gold be 100% synthetic?
2. Is bilingual-ticket-triage (MIT) useful as **negative / adjacent** transfer data, or does support-ticket distribution poison SME order-capture metrics?
3. For commercial path: are CC-BY-NC ASR bases (MMS/Seamless) off-limits, leaving Whisper (MIT) as the only primary open commercial-friendly ASR?
4. Owner Q1–Q3 (native review capacity, consented shops, 90-day goal) — still unanswered; they gate gold quality and timebox.
5. Kaggle Roman-Urdu daily-life intent licence/size — re-fetch when Kaggle is reachable; could it seed shopping/payment/delivery **intent** only?

## What would change my mind

- Discovery of an open (permissive) repo that: (a) parses Roman-Urdu/Urdu SME messages into structured order/payment/credit/delivery fields, (b) ships a versioned eval with field accuracy **and** silent-error rate, (c) is maintained enough to adopt — then mark **gap filled** and park or narrow this project.
- A published paper/dataset card for Roman-Urdu **transaction-line** IE (qty, unit, amount, party placeholder) with ≥200 gold examples under a clear data licence — then mark **gap partly filled** and reuse rather than invent schema from scratch.
- Primary evidence that Whisper (or other MIT/Apache ASR) already meets acceptable WER on consented WhatsApp-quality Urdu notes for number words — would only accelerate N3 timing, **not** close the text-parser gap.

## Recommendation for M0 (N1 only)

Proceed with the hypothesis: build the open parser + validator + gold set. Reuse triage/SETU only as **inspiration or contrastive** examples, not as gold. Keep ASR as a documented adapter landscape; defer measurement to N3. Next spikes remain N4 (privacy) and owner Q1–Q3 — not started here.

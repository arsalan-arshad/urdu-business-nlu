# Spike N4 — Pakistani data-protection rules & draft contributor consent

_Access window: 2026-10-31 · researcher: spike-researcher · phase: M0_  
_No personal data, real chats, or secrets._

> **Entire legal section: not legal advice.** This spike summarises what selected primary texts *say* and what remains *uncertain* for an open eval/dataset of message samples. It does **not** conclude that any project practice is lawful, compliant, or safe. Owner should get qualified legal advice before releasing contributed data or contacting contributors.

## Verdict

**What is clear:** As of access date, no enacted federal *Personal Data Protection Act* was located on Pakistan Code or MoITT’s legislations list (only **draft** Personal Data Protection Bills, latest listed May 2023). Enacted cybercrime law (PECA 2016, as amended) still penalises **unauthorised** access, copying/transmission of data, and unauthorised use of **identity information** — which is why scraping or pasting real chats without clear authorisation is out of scope for this project regardless of the draft bill. The May 2023 **draft** would treat identifiable natural-person data as “personal data,” require consent (with carve-outs), and exclude “anonymized … data which is incapable of identifying an individual” from that definition — but that text is **not** in force.

**What is uncertain for this project:** Whether/when a data-protection statute will be enacted and in what form; how PDA’s Digital Nation Pakistan Act 2025 “data governance / National Data Strategy” powers interact with private open-source datasets; whether synthetic or irreversibly anonymised business-message lines are outside present PECA/ETO risk frames in every edge case; and how third-party messages (a shop contributing *customer* texts) should be consented — **not resolved here, not declared lawful**.

## Legal section (not legal advice) — relevance to storing message samples

### A. Current landscape (high level)

1. **No in-force comprehensive PDP statute located.** MoITT lists the Personal Data Protection Bill as **Draft**. A Senate private member’s 2023 bill record shows it **withdrawn**. Pakistan Code search in this pass did **not** surface an enacted Personal Data Protection Act. Absence of a found statute ≠ permission; other laws still apply.

2. **Constitution Art. 14** protects dignity and, subject to law, privacy of home. It does not spell out open-source eval datasets.

3. **PECA 2016 (as amended)** is the main enacted cybercrime statute reviewed here. For this project’s **DATA_AND_PRIVACY** rules, the practical reading of the *text* is: do not obtain, copy, or transmit other people’s messages or identity information **without authorisation**. Scraping chats, leaking chats, or publishing phone numbers/names/CNICs aligns poorly with the offences described (especially identity information and unauthorised copying/transmission). This spike does **not** map those offences onto any planned release and does **not** opine on criminal liability.

4. **ETO 2002 s.36** addresses unauthorised access to information systems (“violation of privacy of information” heading). It is access-focused, not a civil consent checklist for public datasets.

5. **Digital Nation Pakistan Act 2025** creates PDA and data-governance / National Data Strategy functions. It is **not**, on the face of the Gazette text reviewed, a substitute for the draft Personal Data Protection Bill’s consent/rights regime. Where other laws give specific citizen data rights, those provisions override in the manner the Act states — again, **not applied** here to a go/no-go.

### B. What the May 2023 **draft** PDP Bill says (not in force)

Summarised from the MoITT-headed draft text (ICNL-hosted copy; MoITT PDF URL failed to fetch here):

- **Personal data:** information relating to an identified or identifiable natural person; **anonymized or pseudonymized data incapable of identifying an individual is stated not to be personal data** (draft definition).
- **Consent:** processing generally requires free, specific, informed, unambiguous consent; withdrawal right; controller bears proof burden; other grounds listed (contract, legal obligation, medical/research emergencies, etc.) — exact fit for “public MIT/CC eval set” is **uncertain** and not judged here.
- **Sensitive personal data (draft):** includes (among others) financial information (wording in draft is intricate), health data, CNIC/passport, biometrics, genetic data, religious beliefs, criminal records, political affiliations, caste/tribe, ethnicity.
- **Proposed regulator:** National Commission for Personal Data Protection within six months of commencement (draft).
- **Commencement (draft):** not beyond two years from promulgation, by Gazette notice with advance notice — **irrelevant until enacted**.

**Project implication (policy, not legal conclusion):** Prefer **fully synthetic** samples; for any contributed text, require **explicit consent**, **anonymisation** (names → `PARTY_A`, phones → `PHONE_1`, no CNIC/address/shop identifiers), **minimal fields**, **documented removal**, and **no audio in-repo** — already in `docs/DATA_AND_PRIVACY.md`. Do not release a contributed corpus until **D5 (data licence)** is decided. None of that is a finding of lawfulness.

## Draft consent wording (contributor samples)

> **Draft only. Not legal advice. Not final. Do not use as a signed form until the owner (and preferably counsel) reviews.** Replace bracketed fields. Do not collect more personal data than needed to honour a removal request.

**Working title:** Contributor Sample Consent (urdu-business-nlu eval set) — DRAFT

1. **Who I am.** I am [name or handle]. Contact for removal requests: [email]. I am at least 18 years old.

2. **What I am sharing.** I am offering the following message text(s) (or paraphrases I created) for inclusion in the public **urdu-business-nlu** evaluation / training-adjacent dataset: [paste anonymised text only]. I confirm the text contains **no** real phone numbers, CNICs, home addresses, payment card numbers, or real personal names of living people (placeholders such as `PARTY_A` / `PHONE_1` only).

3. **Ownership / permission.** I confirm that either (a) I authored these messages myself, or (b) I have permission from every person whose identifiable words appear, or (c) the text is **synthetic** and not copied from a real private chat. I have **not** scraped or exported anyone’s chat without authorisation.

4. **Purpose.** I understand the samples may be used to build and publicly evaluate software that parses informal Urdu / Roman-Urdu / mixed business messages into structured fields (orders, payments, credit, delivery).

5. **Public licence.** I understand that if accepted, the sample may be published under the project’s chosen **dataset licence** [D5 undecided — e.g. CC BY 4.0 or CC0 — fill in before use] and may be copied, forked, and mirrored worldwide (including outside Pakistan).

6. **Anonymisation.** I have replaced identifiers with placeholders. I understand the maintainers may further edit or reject samples that look identifying.

7. **Voluntary.** I contribute voluntarily; I am not required to contribute to use the library (when it exists).

8. **Removal.** I can email **arsalanarshad.dev@gmail.com** to request removal of my contributed sample ID(s). I understand removal from **this** repository may not erase every third-party fork or cache already made.

9. **No legal advice / no warranty.** Maintainers do not promise that contribution or publication is lawful in every jurisdiction. I am responsible for ensuring I have the right to share what I submit.

10. **Affirmation.** By typing my name / submitting this form, I agree to the above: ______________________ Date: __________

**Integrator note (not consent):** The library itself must not log, store, or transmit end-user message text unless the integrator configures that — see `docs/DATA_AND_PRIVACY.md`.

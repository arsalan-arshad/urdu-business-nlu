# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, experience level, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

**Examples of behaviour that contributes to a positive environment:**
- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences
- Focusing on what is best for the community
- Showing empathy toward other community members

**Examples of unacceptable behaviour:**
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission
- Pasting real customer chats, phone numbers, names, CNICs or addresses into issues or PRs
- Other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Enforcement

Report concerns to **arsalanarshad.dev@gmail.com**. The maintainer will review and respond as appropriate. Maintainers who do not follow or enforce this Code of Conduct in good faith may be temporarily or permanently removed from the project team.

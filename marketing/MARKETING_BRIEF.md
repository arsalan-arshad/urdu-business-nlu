# Urdu Business NLU — Marketing Brief (seed for a Marketing project)

## Who it is for
Developers, freelancers and small agencies building WhatsApp/ERP/ledger automations for Pakistani and Gulf businesses.

## One-line promise
"Parse informal Urdu business messages without guessing: every number is checked, every doubt goes to a human."

## Positioning
- Open source, offline-capable, no lock-in; the AI only proposes.
- A public eval set so accuracy is measured, not claimed.
- Honest about limits: voice is later; Urdu ASR is weak today.

## Claim guardrails
- No accuracy or "first/only" claims beyond what the eval report shows.
- Do not name or compare against companies without checking current facts.
- Never show real customer messages; anonymised or synthetic only.
- Say plainly that it is a library, not a bot, and needs the integrator's own LLM key if they use the optional adapter.

## Proof assets (once shipped)
- A short clip: ambiguous "kal" caught and sent to confirm.
- The baseline eval table.

## Launch checklist
- [ ] Native-speaker review of all examples
- [ ] Data licence chosen and consent text ready
- [ ] Owner approves any post, repo creation or outreach
